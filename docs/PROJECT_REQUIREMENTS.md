# HAQ — Product Requirements

**HAQ** (हक़, "what is rightfully yours") helps informal workers in India document unpaid-wage and other workplace disputes, send a clear complaint to the employer, and track the response through to settlement and payment.

This is a new, independent implementation written from scratch in this repository. It does not reuse code from any earlier HAQ project.

## Problem

Domestic workers, construction labourers, drivers, delivery riders and other informal workers are often paid late, short or not at all. Their evidence is scattered (WhatsApp screenshots, voice notes, UPI receipts, handwritten attendance) and formal complaint routes assume literacy, legal vocabulary and forms. Employers, in turn, rarely receive a clear, organised claim they can respond to.

## Users

| User | Needs |
|---|---|
| Worker | Explain the problem in their own language (typing or speaking), attach whatever evidence they have, get a clear complaint, decide whether to send it, follow the response, accept or reject offers, confirm payment. |
| Employer | See only claims addressed to them, understand what is asked, review shared evidence, reply (text or file), offer a settlement, record payment. |

## Goals

1. A worker with limited digital skills can create and send a claim on a phone without help.
2. Nothing is sent to an employer without the worker's explicit approval.
3. Employers see only claims and evidence explicitly shared with them.
4. Money arithmetic is deterministic and never done by an AI model.
5. The AI never invents facts; unknowns are marked; every important fact is linked to its source.
6. The system never claims something succeeded (upload, submission, payment) unless the server confirmed it.

## Non-goals (this version)

- Acting as a court, giving legal advice, or citing legal provisions.
- Declaring evidence or people fraudulent.
- Processing online payments.
- Native mobile apps (the web app is mobile-first instead).

## Functional requirements

### Accounts (R-A)
- R-A1 Workers and employers register with email + password through **separate endpoints**; the role is assigned by the server, never taken from the request body.
- R-A2 Email verification by 6-digit code. Workers can draft before verifying but must verify to submit. Employers must verify to see any claim (proves ownership of the email that claims are addressed to).
- R-A3 Signing in to the wrong portal is refused with a clear message.
- R-A4 Password reset by emailed code. Reset invalidates existing sessions.
- R-A5 Sign out of all devices.
- R-A6 Employer identity verification is a separate status (`unverified` by default) and is never implied by registration.

### Claims (R-C)
- R-C1 Guided flow: describe → evidence → key questions → summary & checks → complaint draft → review & send.
- R-C2 Text or voice input (browser speech recognition where available); transcript is always editable.
- R-C3 Draft is saved on the server and, before that, locally on the device (with a clear "delete local draft" control).
- R-C4 Optional link to a work record; work records are never required.
- R-C5 Complaint draft generated by the configured AI provider (IBM Granite via watsonx.ai or Ollama) or a deterministic template when no AI is configured. The provider used is shown to the worker.
- R-C6 Worker can edit the complete complaint text and choose which evidence files are shared.
- R-C7 Submission requires an explicit confirmation flag, a verified email, an employer email and non-empty complaint text. Submission is idempotent.
- R-C8 Statuses follow a server-side state machine (see ARCHITECTURE.md).
- R-C9 Worker can withdraw, reply to questions, accept/reject offers, confirm or dispute payments, close the claim as resolved, and export a printable record.

### Evidence (R-E)
- R-E1 Accept JPG/PNG/WebP, PDF, DOCX, TXT, WAV/MP3/M4A/OGG/WebM audio, MP4/MOV video.
- R-E2 Validate extension, declared type, file signature and size; reject mismatches.
- R-E3 Store originals privately under generated names; record SHA-256, size, uploader, time, claim.
- R-E4 Extract text in the background: TXT, PDF text, DOCX text, image OCR (if Tesseract is installed). Audio/video are stored but marked "not transcribed" (no speech-to-text service configured). Failures are reported, never hidden.
- R-E5 Extract amounts and dates; offer them as suggestions only.
- R-E6 Worker can correct extracted text.
- R-E7 Evidence is never deleted or altered because of a check result.

### Checks (R-K)
- R-K1 Rule-based findings classified as: Information matches / Possible inconsistency / Needs manual review / Unable to verify / Extraction failed.
- R-K2 Every finding names the file(s) and the reason. No fraud labels, no scores.

### Employer (R-M)
- R-M1 Inbox of claims addressed to the employer's verified email, paginated.
- R-M2 Acknowledge, reply, ask a question, attach files, offer settlement, record payment with optional receipt.
- R-M3 Employer cannot edit the claim, the complaint, or any evidence.
- R-M4 Employer recording a payment does not resolve the claim; the worker must confirm it.

### Wages & work records (R-W)
- R-W1 Calculator for daily, hourly, weekly, monthly, piece-rate and task-based pay: expected = rate × units; outstanding = max(expected − paid, 0); excess = max(paid − expected, 0). Decimal arithmetic, 2 dp, half-up rounding, validated ranges.
- R-W2 Result labelled as an arithmetic estimate, not a legal finding.
- R-W3 Work records CRUD for the worker's own records.

### Notifications & history (R-N)
- R-N1 Timeline of claim events with actor and time.
- R-N2 In-app notifications for meaningful events, de-duplicated.
- R-N3 Security audit log for sign-ins, failures, password resets.

### Languages & accessibility (R-L)
- R-L1 Main screens, voice typing and letter headings in 13 languages (English, Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Urdu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi and Assamese); Urdu shown right-to-left. Any other language can be typed or spoken and is kept word-for-word ("Another language"). Translations need native-speaker review.
- R-L2 Large touch targets, readable contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, labelled inputs.
